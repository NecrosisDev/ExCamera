Add VR, view effects, wounds and fire; move settings into Mod Settings

- Dying no longer freezes the game: nav, swap, cursor and aim hooks release
  cameraLock/aimLock in __finally when a read of the dying character faults.
- Blood and decals stop z-fighting in first person with supersampling on: the
  depth correction keeps authored P10 offsets at 16 depth steps or more.
- View effects (SSR, DoF, AO) via a sampled-views detour, and once on the
  averaged image under supersampling; admitted only on whole-function
  byte-exact contracts. Temporal AO and reflections, depth of field at half
  resolution, eye adaptation, edge smoothing, sharpening under supersampling,
  helmet view, player silhouette through occluders, hurt/winded overlay.
- Post-FX 25-30: cheaper occlusion/reflections (a quarter of the view above
  1200 px, bounce from three taps, then off as PROVISIONAL after torchlight
  lines); per-stage GPU/CPU frame timing in the status; one sync point per
  frame for the meter and focus readbacks; NaN/Inf-safe histories and stray
  texel clamping (no solid squares on grates); effect gotchas.
- Screen effects keep drawing with the inventory open; they skip only while
  Settings is open. Desktop path copies colour and depth with one blit into
  textures of the window's depth format (copyTexSubImage took the driver's slow
  path). Auto quality tier: smaller effect targets while frames run over 18 ms.
- Rename character balance/balanceMax to stamina/staminaMax.
- OpenXR VR, compiled only with build.py --openxr-sdk: per-eye effects row,
  stab modifier, mirror mode, comfort vignette, grip/haptics/panel tuning,
  stereo title menu, laser pointer, wrist menu and toggles, runtime-loss
  recovery, recenter memory, weapon feel, seated height, grab turning,
  frame-time readout, game hand mesh with finger poses and shape curl,
  tracked grabs and per-item grip offsets, VR kick (motionPlay 0x179EE0).
  VR settings cut from 32 rows to 19; the VR category exists only in VR builds.
- Finger bones driven on the game's own hand mesh (xr_bones.inl), posed from
  the held item in first and third person for every character; dead hands go
  limp, gauntleted hands stay still. Fingertip index fixed (an arena practice
  crash, stack cookie in trackedBoneDriveHeld). Skin streams are matched on
  their format's low 24 bits (the game's flag byte had stopped finger poses,
  skinned helmet hiding and VR hands).
- Helmets: hidden by whole worn piece (rims and aventails too); Helmet view
  follows the real helmet's shape and shades a soft opening when it is hidden.
- Held-item detail (procedural) and held 31-37: surfaces per material (steel,
  wood, leather, cloth); replay draws capped and nearest first within 6 m;
  level and world-object materials with a far tier (desktop only); the two
  strongest nearby game lights tint level detail; per-sector light-list cache
  and the held mask reused on missed frames (no 10 Hz or weapon-blood flicker).
- Wounds 04-14: cuts, stabs and bruises on bodies where weapons got through,
  blood runs and drips that dry, metal takes smears only, outer layers inferred
  from geometry; wound skins baked into per-character copies of the body
  diffuse; their own screen pass; fractures from Injuries' broken bones, burns
  and burn scars, healing into scars (Regenerate's pace when marked); severed
  limbs with stump and end caps; stale sever-cache entries dropped by node,
  actor and bits.
- Stone skin (stone_skin.inl) and the shared face-split draw.
- Fire (fire.inl): the Fire mod's fire nodes, burning people and a flaming
  blade drawn as volumes; a fire node on the blade is not drawn twice.
- Smooth lighting (on, from the next level load): a glShaderSource detour
  rewrites the game's VoxelGI reads into a tricubic B-spline sample, so strong
  lights (Hellmode's red torches) fall off round instead of in squares; a
  rewrite that does not compile on the driver leaves the game's source.
- Performance: draw distance (small props and far characters past a
  radius-scaled cutoff, with hysteresis) and Lights per object (the strongest N
  of the game's six); a 32-entry per-frame VirtualQuery region cache. The light
  trims are put back at the end of the same frame (the swap), never the next:
  a round change frees the meshes between frames. Torch shadows are measured
  (shadow_renders, shadow_ms via the 0x4F4A0 scheduler); a turn-taking shadow
  budget was tried and removed (pooled maps showed other lights' shadows).
- Settings: every ExCamera setting moved into Mod Settings by category
  (Camera, Graphics, Combat, Controls, VR) with sub-headers; a left category
  list and a right details pane, one setting per line; optional by-mod view
  (ModSettingsByMod); hover help; short names with descriptive tooltips;
  effects as 0-100% sliders, on/off as named buttons; slider values update
  while dragging; the wheel and scroll thumb work in the pane; Defaults resets
  the shown category. manifest.toml ships beside the DLL and in the ZIP (EMTK
  skips a mod folder without one); Hellmode's 1066-high form widens; kind-1
  buttons act without the transient bit; the Settings host keeps no runtime
  label pointers past rows().
- Crash safety: the Settings tick, held-item bookkeeping and the drag-drop door
  check fall back to the game on a fault. fault-log.txt beside the DLL names
  each distinct exception (code, address, ExCamera frame, three game frames);
  repeats are only counted. A wound whose character was freed is retired
  instead of faulting every frame. spawn-log.txt records the first ten seconds
  after each spawn.
- Hellmode EXE accepted as a verified build; notice banner text wraps.
- build.py --dev: readiness file and dev-load.ini save-slot loader, never packaged.
- Regenerated signature and contract tables (233 targets, 763 signatures,
  153 owners, 356 witnesses, 277 field declarations).
